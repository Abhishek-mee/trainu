USB tips

  • One-tap USB: connect the cable, enable USB debugging, then press Connect phone in the laptop panel and open localhost on this phone.
  • USB tethering: turn on USB tethering in the phone's hotspot settings; the laptop appears as a wired network.
  • Wi-Fi works too and stays fully offline — just keep both devices on the same router.
  • Keep the laptop awake while watching. Closing the lid pauses playback.
